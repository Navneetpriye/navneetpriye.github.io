Most freelancers are Excel developers who learned about finance. I am a finance operations professional who mastered Excel — and that distinction changes everything for my clients. I understand your workflows, your compliance pressures, and your operational realities before I write a single formula.

I offer two focused service lines: FinOps Excel Studio for Indian financial companies seeking custom Excel automation, and MortgageOps India for US mortgage lenders and brokers needing remote QC and back-office support.
